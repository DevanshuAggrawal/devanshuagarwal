Leadership philosophy

I lead by building the system, not just the team: clear quality standards, automation and fast feedback loops that let engineers own their outcomes. I invest in people through mentoring and clear scope, so decisions move closer to the work. I measure what matters to the business (release confidence, cycle time and defect escape) and keep those numbers visible to executives.

Technical focus: test automation (Playwright, Karate, Gatling), performance and NFT, CI/CD on Jenkins and OpenShift, AWS and Terraform, observability with Dynatrace and Prometheus, and LLM-based QE tooling.
